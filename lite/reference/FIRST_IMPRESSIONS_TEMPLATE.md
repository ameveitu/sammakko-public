## Suggested short pilot

Use a short exploratory session suited to the participant. Ask a child who meets the stated reading/device assumptions to start, choose something, try an activity and explore another place. Include a fast jump when appropriate and observe a mistake/help/return path if it arises naturally. Do not manufacture failure merely to fill the form.

A teacher can review one familiar topic and one progression across prerequisites. A tester can reproduce the concrete scenarios in `ACCEPTANCE.md`.

The observer should record what happened before proposing an explanation. If the observer intervenes, record the help; do not describe the outcome as independent.

## Child impressions

- What did you think you were supposed to do?
- What was enjoyable?
- Was anything confusing or annoying?
- Could you find where you wanted to go?
- Did the help make sense?
- Would you choose to play another activity? Which one?
- Lentopaja (if played): What was the workshop for? What did the backpack do? Why did you pick those wings? What happened in the flight?

Record the child's actual words only when supplied. Otherwise use a clearly attributed paraphrase.

## Teacher review

- Does the learner action match the stated KC and its declared MVP domain?
- Can a learner succeed without using the intended skill?
- Are prompt, representation, answer and explanation correct?
- Does the difficulty come from the target skill, reading, or controls?
- Are hints useful and honestly separated from unassisted checking?
- Are inference and deferred checks described fairly?
- What is the smallest lesson change likely to improve this activity?
