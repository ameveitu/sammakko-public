The attached masterplan, visual plan and eepos describe Sulka as an established researcher who provides tools and questions, then leaves room for the learner. They do not approve invented new characters. Source hashes are in metadata.json.

Match the existing silhouette and restrained warm matte material language. Use a small armature and separate idle, in-place walk, gentle gesture and gentle nod actions. Origin at the ground, metres, Blender +X forward; GLB +Y up and +X forward.”
