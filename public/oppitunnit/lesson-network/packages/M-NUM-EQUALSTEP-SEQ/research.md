Cardinality and Counting
https://www.ncetm.org.uk/classroom-resources/ey-cardinality-and-counting/
Read: Number words, backward counting, count tagging, cardinal total and conservation sections.
Design: Keep a sequence location distinct from a count of objects. Support backward boundary crossings and starts other than1. Group diagrams explicitly distinguish number of groups from number of equal units.

DfE Mathematics guidance: year1, 1NPV–1 and1NF–2
https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/600952a5e90e073ed5001da7/Maths_guidance_year_1.pdf
