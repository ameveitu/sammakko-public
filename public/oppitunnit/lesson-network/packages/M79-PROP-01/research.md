Luettu29.10.2026. Distinguish part-to-part ratio from part-to-whole fraction and preserve both quantities by the same scale factor. https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/6-5-solve-proportions-and-their-applications
Luettu29.10.2026. Proportion as equality of two ratios; exact cross-products as corroborating check with nonzero denominators. Solving general variable equations is beyond this concept boundary.
