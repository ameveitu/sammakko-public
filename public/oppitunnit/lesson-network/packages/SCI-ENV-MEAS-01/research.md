NCETM's Early Years Measures distinguishes the measured attribute and equal repeating units. This supplies an optional foundation bridge, not an age-level recommendation for grades3–6. Oak's ruler lesson explicitly addresses aligning with the ruler edge instead of zero. We retain that error but supply an original 1→6 versus0→5 comparison and a keyboard-operable repositioning model. Its shorthand about ruler numbers is not generalized to all scales: intervals carry the measurement.

Oak's Temperature lesson explicitly varies thermometer scales. Our thermometer has 2°C intervals and an exact18°C tick (worked example14°C). This transfers the equal-interval idea while avoiding an unintroduced negative/decimal temperature. Oak's Year4 Thermometers and data loggers pairs tool use with recording; only its ambient-temperature context is used. The wording that temperature measures an object's “heat” is not adopted: heat and temperature are different concepts. Its warm/cold water and fragile-glass activities are excluded.

DfE Working scientifically couples equipment, standard units and contextual observations, and introduces appropriate repeated readings. MetLink/Royal Meteorological Society supplies upper-primary thermometer reading followed by real observation at a stated place/time. We adopt the practice-to-real-observation distinction; we reject hot-water tasks and hourly data gathering as unnecessary scope here. MetLink identifies the page as reproduced with Met Office permission; that permission is not transferred to this project.

Lite's repeatScene visibly mispoints the second reading and then crosses the odd result out. Our optional repeated-measurement discussion instead preserves original readings, checks procedure/unit/endpoints and repeats. Agreement alone does not establish truth or identify which reading caused an error.
